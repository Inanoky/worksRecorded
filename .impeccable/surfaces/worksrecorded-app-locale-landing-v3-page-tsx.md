# Landing V3 Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

- Target: `worksRecorded/app/[locale]/Landing_V3/page.tsx`
- Mode: Persuade
- This is an additive experiment; existing Landing and Landing_v2 routes remain untouched.

## Audience, job, and action

- Audience: owners and project managers at Latvian construction companies.
- Job: understand how familiar WhatsApp updates become reliable project records, cost visibility, and BIS-ready documentation.
- Primary action: book a demo through the existing Calendly link.

## Proof and constraints

- Use only real product screens and approved customer assets already in the repository.
- Do not use the unsubstantiated 4.8/5 rating or invent testimonials, metrics, prices, or customer claims.
- Preserve WorksRecorded naming, wordmark treatment, WhatsApp centrality, localization, and the green/white/dark-neutral identity.

## Chosen direction

- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/landing-v3-dashboard-control.png`.
- Supporting approved section comps:
  - `.impeccable/mocks/landing-v3-section-whatsapp-ai-option-c.png`
  - `.impeccable/mocks/landing-v3-section-budget-option-c.png`
- Direction: dashboard-led construction review. A narrow copy rail sits beside oversized real product evidence. Fine green leader lines and numbered annotations trace field input into structured records and management decisions.
- Memorable moment: the WhatsApp phone overlaps the large dashboard while a three-stage evidence path connects message, record, and cost visibility.

## Component grammar

- Corners: mostly square or subtly rounded (4-12px); device frames may follow their real hardware radius.
- Lines: 1px forest/green technical leaders, dashed construction baselines, small circular endpoints.
- Elevation: restrained and reserved for physical devices; content remains matte and flat.
- Type: bold compact grotesk headlines, calm workhorse body text, condensed uppercase annotations.
- Color fields: white/mineral-green working surface, deep forest-green sectional anchors, charcoal typography, WhatsApp green only for active routes.
